## Design note: why status is outcome-based, not age-based (checker revision)

RESEARCH Pattern 1 (verified in next@16.4.3 `unstable-cache.ts`): on a stale entry `unstable_cache` re-runs the callback; in request context it returns the stale value immediately and revalidates in the background; only in ISR `prerender-legacy` does the render await the refresh (Assumption A12, MEDIUM on Vercel). So after an idle period a regenerated page, `/api/weather`, or plan 05's `router.refresh()` can legitimately receive an entry older than TTL + grace with no upstream failure at all. Age-based `stale` would then show the false "No pudimos actualizar…" notice.

Chosen design (also from RESEARCH Pattern 1 facts): (a) a cache **miss** in `unstable_cache` is always awaited and its error propagates; (b) the key includes `JSON(args)`; (c) nested fetches inside `unstable_cache` are forced no-store **without** marking the page dynamic. Therefore a second getter keyed by a 15-min time bucket, `getOpenMeteoForecastFresh(Math.floor(nowMs / 900_000))`, is a guaranteed foreground fetch the first time it is read in each bucket, shared by every later render in that bucket, and safe inside an ISR page. `readThrough` reads the last-good entry first; only if that entry is older than TTL does it call the bucketed getter. Success → fresh data, status `ok`. Failure → last-good data, status `stale`, `errorCode` = the real SourceError code. Miss on the last-good getter + failure → rejected → `error` (Sin datos). Cost: at most one extra Open-Meteo call per 15-min bucket, and only when the last-good entry was found past TTL (well inside the Pitfall 12 budget). A direct un-cached `fetchOpenMeteoForecastRaw()` call from the render is forbidden: a no-store fetch outside `unstable_cache` would turn `/` dynamic (or fail the build).

MET Norway does NOT get the read-through: its ToS forbids repeating requests before `Expires` (Pitfall 7) and MET is never displayed in phase 1. Its status is `ok` when fulfilled and `error` when rejected; its age stays visible through `fetchedAt`.
